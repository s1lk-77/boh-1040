# Playtest Report 10/31/2026

Participant name: kkb.

## Feedback Questions

### Design

- Do you think this app will be useful for Assassins' Guild? If not, what can I do to make it more helpful?
  - A: lots of cool things don't get used. App has the potential to be useful but wants UX flow improvements. We spent 30 minutes discussing this and I did not get to the rest of the questions, but her feedback on this question was invaluable.
- When would you want to use something like this, if ever?
- Would you be interested in using this as a GM? Why or why not?
- Favorite / least favorite part?
- Something you would change? + Feature requests.

### Technical

- Were there any technical issues?
  - A: On kkb's first attempt to create a bag, her internet connection wavered, and it rejected her request. I had thought it was a bug until she tried again. We need beter error messaging to distinguish between a network error and a user error.
- Did the buttons do what you think they did?

### UI

- What was the most intuitive part of the UI? The most confusing?
  - A: on task "remove a player," she clicked the player button a few times before realizing the delete button existed with the multiselect button. On the ability deletion, she used the editor delete button. On ability add, she used the editor add and had many thoughts on data upload pipelines.
- Were the help buttons helpful and do you wish there was more?
- What would you change about the looks of this app?
- Did the buttons do what you think they did? Were the icons intuitive?
  - A: she attempted to click the filter icon, which was not a button. She expected that to display a popover where she could filter by category.

## Reflection

Overall, kkb was able to navigate the interface as intended. Her only question to me was "what should I put for my name?", and after I encouraged her to click around, she was able to find the help button. She used the help button on both login pages, but did not use it otherwise. The test went very smoothly, suggesting that the current design of BoH is well suited for its functionality.

Surprisingly, during the test, our longest discussion was over color and her complaint that green was not an option. We also went on a detour to debate what shade of pink was conventional for an ability card. I had certainly not considered that this would be an issue, but it makes sense that the perception of what is conventional would differ across individuals.

The most helpful feedback she provided was our discussion at the end. While BoH seemed intuitive enough for the functionality it currently supports, kkb pointed out that there is still a considerable amount of road to cover between it solving a few small problems to being a universalizable tool. Specifically, she addressed three bad situations she foresees this would generate in terms of player and GM friction, all of which I agree with and address [in this document](./issues.md). She is a far more experienced GM than me, and I trust that her unvarnished opinions will guide the next step of BoH in the correct direction.
