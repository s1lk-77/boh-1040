# Bag of Holding (6.1040)

_Tired of holding 50 pieces of paper in your sweaty hands and emptying out four pockets just to find that one elusive ability card you really wished you knew where you put? Fear no more. The Wizard of the Court hath forth... with an offering. A Bag of Holding. The price, you ask? Only that you use your powers for good and **never for evil.**_

---

Live action roleplay (LARP) claims its standing as a one-of-a-kind genre in the cosmos of games for it being so incredibly immersive. LARP games put you right into their world, physically, using a combination of cleverly demarcated real-life spaces and the vast remainder of your imagination. Here at MIT Assassins' Guild, our signature LARP style is the information-dense, mechanics-heavy "secrets & powers" games. These games feature _a lot_ of text, a novel's worth at times, with up to hundreds of items and player abilities. Traditionally, game pieces such as abilities are packaged as card-sized physical cutouts, which have not only been labor intensive to produce, but also adversarial to pocket space and difficult to inventory. Especially when a game is ten days long...

_Bag of Holding_ is an attempt to convert a universal gadget we now call the phone into a digital pocket for your ability cards. It won't be as cool as the physical one, we know, but we hope to get as close as we can to it while saving you all the trouble!
